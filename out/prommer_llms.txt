# Thomas Prommer

> Technology executive (CTO/CIO) and AI strategy consultant, writing from practice on technology leadership and on hybrid endurance training — marathon, Ironman and HYROX.

Two content pillars, both written first-hand rather than aggregated:

- **Tech** — technology leadership, AI strategy, the emerging Chief Technology & AI Officer role, interim leadership, executive search, and compensation benchmarks.
- **Training** — hybrid athlete training, with the deepest coverage on HYROX: what it is, how it compares to CrossFit, race preparation, and city-by-city training guidance.

Content is published in English with substantial translated coverage in German, Spanish, French, Portuguese and Italian, plus partial coverage in Dutch, Finnish, Swedish, Polish, Indonesian, Danish and Czech. English is the source of truth; translated pages mirror it under a language prefix (`/de/`, `/es/`, …).
